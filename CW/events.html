Spring Planting Festival

🕐 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM 📍 Main Garden

Kick off the growing season with our annual planting festival! Enjoy free seed packs, planting demonstrations, live music, and refreshments. Bring the whole family.

Free Entry

Composting Masterclass

🕐 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM 📍 Education Area

Learn the science behind composting with our expert Marcus. Topics include hot composting, worm farms, and bokashi bins. Materials provided — just bring your curiosity.

Workshop

Earth Day Community Clean-Up

🕐 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM 📍 Garden & Surrounding Streets

Wildflower Seed Bomb Workshop

Family Friendly

Summer Solstice BBQ & Open Garden
